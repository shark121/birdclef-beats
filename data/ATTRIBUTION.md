# Attribution

The audio behind this data is the **BirdCLEF+ 2026** training set
(Kaggle competition `birdclef-2026`, Cornell Lab of Ornithology and partners),
itself drawn from **Xeno-canto** (23,043 recordings) and **iNaturalist**
(12,506 recordings). No audio is redistributed here; the files in `data/` are
metadata, derived clip lists and metrics.

Every recording keeps its own licence and author. `recordings.csv` lists, per row,
the `recordist`, the `license` and the original `source_url`; that table is the
attribution record.

| licence | recordings |
|---|---|
| CC BY-NC-SA | 23,092 |
| CC BY-NC | 9,651 |
| CC BY | 2,202 |
| CC BY-SA | 314 |
| CC0 | 290 |

Most recordings are NonCommercial (NC) and/or ShareAlike (SA): use them, and
anything derived from them, non-commercially, and release derivatives under
compatible terms.
